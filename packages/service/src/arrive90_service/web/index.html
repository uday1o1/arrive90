        Methodology

        One frozen comparison, complete uncertainty

        Arrive90 compares a 0.90-target, 20-minute-cap policy with the static fastest candidate on identical queries.

        Unresolved outcomes remain in calibration and policy bounds, and uncertainty uses at least 2,000 complete-service-day bootstrap replicates.

        Only the selected card may display a validated probability or quantile; comparator, alternative, backup, and recovery outputs remain visibly unavailable.

        Machine-readable Milestone 6 evidence card

        Limitations

        What this interface does not claim

        • The displayed probability is an estimate, never a guarantee.
        • The current public source cannot identify the required Vehicle Position boarding evidence, so no MBTA reliability recommendation is accepted.
        • Prospective live calibration is pending.
        • Recovery is a schedule action and receives no new deadline probability or reliability-target status.
        • The scope is station to station, with no rider identity, coordinates, or location history retained.
